🍽️ Zomato Restaurant Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

📌 Project Overview

The Zomato Restaurant Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze restaurant distribution, customer ratings, cuisine popularity, pricing, and service availability.

The dashboard transforms restaurant data into meaningful visual insights using KPIs, bar charts, and donut charts. It helps users explore restaurant trends across cities, rating categories, price ranges, and online ordering and table-booking services.

🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze the total number of restaurants and their distribution.
- Understand average restaurant ratings and customer voting activity.
- Identify cities with the highest restaurant counts.
- Discover the most common cuisines.
- Compare average restaurant ratings across price ranges.
- Evaluate online delivery and table-booking availability.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Power BI Desktop – Dashboard development and visualization
- Power Query – Data cleaning and preparation, if applied
- DAX – Measures and aggregations, where used
- Data Visualization – KPI cards, bar charts, and donut charts

📈 Visualizations & Analysis

1. Restaurant Distribution by Rating Category

Shows how restaurants are distributed across rating categories, helping identify the most common rating groups.

2. Top 10 Cities by Restaurant Count

Compares restaurant counts across cities to identify major restaurant markets. New Delhi is the leading city in the displayed chart.

3. Top 10 Cuisines

Highlights the most frequently represented cuisines. North Indian cuisine leads the displayed ranking, followed by categories such as North Indian–Chinese and Chinese.

4. Average Restaurant Rating by Price Range

Compares average ratings across four price ranges. The displayed averages generally increase from approximately 2.2 in the lowest range to around 3.4 in the highest range.

5. Online Delivery Availability

Displays the split between restaurants offering and not offering online delivery. Approximately 74.34% fall into the larger category, while 25.66% fall into the smaller category; the legend should be checked to confirm which percentage corresponds to Yes or No.

6. Table Booking Availability

Shows table-booking availability, with approximately 87.88% in the larger category and 12.12% in the smaller category. Confirm the Yes/No mapping from the legend before interpreting the split.

7. Interactive Filters

Country Code and City slicers enable users to filter the report and explore restaurant trends for selected locations.

🔍 Key Findings

- The dataset contains approximately 10,000 restaurants.
- The average restaurant rating is 2.67.
- Customer voting activity totals approximately one million votes.
- New Delhi has the highest restaurant count among the cities displayed.
- North Indian cuisine is the leading cuisine category in the displayed ranking.
- Average ratings increase across the displayed price ranges.
